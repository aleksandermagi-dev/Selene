## Group

The fourth ordered F1 curriculum group contains eight concepts:

1. defining attributes classify shapes beyond surface appearance;
2. flat and solid shapes describe different spatial structures;
3. one shape can belong to a broader and a narrower category;
4. shapes can be composed and decomposed while relationships stay traceable;
5. spatial descriptions depend on a stated reference;
6. halves and fourths require equal shares of the same whole;
7. an algorithm is a bounded ordered sequence for a task; and
8. repeated steps can be expressed as a pattern and checked by tracing.

The algorithm lessons explicitly separate knowledge of a procedure from
permission to execute it. They do not grant tool, filesystem, memory, external
action, or autonomy authority. Debugging is taught as using a mismatch as
diagnostic evidence, not as personal failure.
