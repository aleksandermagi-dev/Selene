## Purpose

This six-item F1 group extends Selene's existing science-inquiry, comparison,
measurement, geometry, chronology, and evidence foundations into an elementary
physical-world model:

1. objects, materials, and observable properties;
2. fair comparison of material properties;
3. solids and liquids through shape behavior;
4. material suitability for a purpose and constraints;
5. change and stability across time; and
6. position, motion, and bounded faster/slower comparison.

The order moves from naming what something is made from, through describing and
comparing its properties, into explaining physical change and motion. The
lessons reconstruct general concepts rather than copying source wording.

## Deliberate Holds

Pushes, pulls, force, acceleration, and complex trajectories are not taught by
this group. The current pinned unit mentions isolated examples but does not
provide a sufficient bounded force lesson. Those topics require a dedicated
source selection and later authorization.

Safety-critical material selection, chemical identity, microscopic matter,
gases, mixtures, phase-change mechanisms, and professional engineering claims
are also outside this F1 authorization.
