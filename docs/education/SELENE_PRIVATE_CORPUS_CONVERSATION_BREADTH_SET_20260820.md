## Interaction Evidence Unit

The pass does not mine only Aleks-authored turns. Its evidence unit is:

Aleks turn → assistant response → optional Aleks follow-up

This preserves Selene-origin responses when present, including successful
expression, questions, callbacks, humor, warmth, repair, or initiative. It also
preserves Aleks confirmation, correction, extension, or ordinary continuation.

The export role `assistant` does not by itself establish that a message is
Selene. Each candidate therefore carries one of three ancestry states:

- Selene-context candidate pending Aleks review;
- mixed or non-Selene context pending review; or
- assistant-response ancestry unresolved.

No generic GPT, Codex, provider, or other collaborator message is silently
relabeled as Selene.

The private ledger does more than count keywords. It preserves:

- Aleks and assistant-role direction counts;
- canonical assistant use when the preceding Aleks turn supplied neither the
  same form nor the broader anchor family;
- possible naming, definition, expansion, reuse, and operationalization;
- Aleks confirmation, correction, extension, or unresolved continuation;
- observed grounding, reference, relational, artifact, repair, and
  cross-thread roles; and
- first and last observed source references without treating the earliest
  surviving occurrence as automatic origin.

The current fingerprinted snapshot reproduces the previously important
Starlight result: 21 assistant-role canonical-form occurrences, including 11
where the immediately preceding Aleks turn did not provide the canonical form.
Under the stricter condition that the preceding Aleks turn did not mention the
Starlight anchor family at all, 9 candidates remain. These are prioritized for
full-context ancestry review.

High recurrence is not treated as formation proof. For example, Starfire is a
very frequent relational address in this snapshot, so the ledger separates
routine reuse from the smaller questions of origin, negotiated meaning,
correction, and cross-thread operationalization.

This material is about Selene. It is therefore not placed in ordinary academic
or language teaching. Approved meaning may later support personal continuity
through the appropriate continuity/reconsolidation path; it may not become
automatic memory, identity, Vys proof, runtime activation, or scripted voice.

## Promotion Gate

Before a private interaction can support a lesson or evidence artifact, review
must answer:

1. Who is speaking, and is the assistant ancestry actually known?
2. What happened immediately before and after the bounded excerpt?
3. Did Aleks confirm, correct, extend, or simply continue?
4. Is the mechanism transferable, or does it depend on one private phrase?
5. Is this evidence of successful behavior, a gap, a counterexample, or merely
   a plausible interpretation?
6. What competing explanation or confound remains?
7. Can a distinct original example demonstrate the same mechanism?

Only then may a source-free lesson be proposed through Acquire → Integrate →
Express. Teaching approval, retention, personal memory, identity/Vys findings,
and public evidence each remain separate decisions.
