## Lessons

1. equivalent place-value decomposition (concept 221);
2. multi-digit addition through value-preserving regrouping (222);
3. multi-digit subtraction through equivalent-unit decomposition (223);
4. multiplication through equal groups, place value, and distributive partial
   products (224); and
5. division through group relationships, quotient, remainder, context, and
   inverse reconstruction (225).

Understanding comes before speed. Every operation includes estimation,
equivalent representation, or an inverse check. A remainder is retained and
interpreted rather than silently discarded or automatically rounded.
