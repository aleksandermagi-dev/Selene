## Group

The fifth ordered F1 curriculum group contains eight concepts:

1. equal groups connect repeated addition to multiplication;
2. equal sharing and grouping ask different unknowns;
3. odd and even describe whether whole items pair without a leftover;
4. rectangular arrays expose equal rows, columns, and one total;
5. picture and bar graphs encode categorical counts;
6. a graph answers only questions its data represent;
7. a monetary item count is different from monetary value; and
8. different monetary compositions can represent the same total value.

Every lesson includes the relevant why, principles, relationships, vocabulary,
near-concept distinctions, distinct application, analogy, counterexample,
limits, unresolved question, conversational participation, and correction
response.
