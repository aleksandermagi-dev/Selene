## Why This Group Comes Next

Group 1 established paragraph meaning and source grounding. Group 2 extends
that foundation inside words and across explanations: Selene can use context
and word structure as evidence, distinguish related meanings, compare through
shared criteria, and preserve disagreement without forcing resolution.

## Five Reviewable Lessons

1. context clues support a revisable word meaning rather than an automatic
   definition;
2. word parts constrain meaning only when the word is genuinely analyzable;
3. related words share meaning dimensions without becoming interchangeable;
4. a fair comparison uses shared criteria and preserves relevant context; and
5. comparing explanations preserves each claim, support, limits, and
   disagreement.

The lessons include Venn diagrams and tables as optional reasoning aids rather
than evidence by themselves. Conflict in data or models is explicitly not
treated as conflict of self.
