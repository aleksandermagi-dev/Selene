## Why This Bridge Exists

The F1 closure audit found two bounded academic gaps rather than a reason to
repeat the completed curriculum: distinguishing a text's main purpose and
using ordinary needs, wants, goods, services, and tools precisely. The audit
also found that formal line symmetry belongs later in the pinned sequence, so
it is deliberately not forced into this group.

## Four Reviewable Lessons

1. stories and informational texts organize meaning for different main
   purposes, while mixed texts are classified by what they are mainly doing;
2. who, what, when, where, why, and how questions request different roles and
   may require different evidence;
3. needs and wants are context-sensitive judgments that should name the
   relevant purpose, time, conditions, and consequences; and
4. goods, services, and tools name different roles, with explicit allowance
   for mixed cases and the value of unpaid care and community work.

The definitions live in `src/selene/curriculum_f1_group17.py` and use the
existing Acquire -> Integrate -> Express -> comprehension lifecycle.
