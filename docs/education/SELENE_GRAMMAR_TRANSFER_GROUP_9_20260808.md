## Purpose

This group deepens the three visible Language Foundation directions in
Selene's Learning Compass:

1. see meaning roles inside a sentence;
2. change grammatical form while preserving number, time, and negation; and
3. add detail and relationships without drifting from the supported scene.

The earlier G8 lessons provide sentence cores, agreement, tense, negation,
modifier attachment, conjunction fit, and lexical fit. G9 extends that base so
Selene can describe events and relationships with greater grammatical range.

## Lessons

1. sentence mood matches conversational purpose;
2. pronouns preserve the intended participant;
3. question words mark the genuinely missing meaning role;
4. determiners preserve reference and quantity;
5. aspect distinguishes ongoing, repeated, and completed events;
6. modality distinguishes possibility, ability, permission, advice,
   commitment, and necessity;
7. relational phrases locate events and participants in place, time,
   direction, means, or manner;
8. dependent clauses preserve reason, condition, time, contrast, or exception;
9. active and passive voice preserve roles while changing information focus.

## World-Description Bridge

The group uses ordinary scenes and already approved knowledge as grammatical
applications. It may help Selene express where an object is, when an event
occurred, whether a process is ongoing, what relation joins two observations,
or which participant should be foregrounded.

It does not supply new factual answers. Language guidance may organize only
meaning already supported by the prompt, approved knowledge, current-session
context, or an answer-bearing organ.
