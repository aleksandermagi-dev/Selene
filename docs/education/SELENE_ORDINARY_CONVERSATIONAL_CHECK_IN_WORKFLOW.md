# Selene Ordinary Conversational Check-In

Status: active lightweight workflow

## Purpose

The Ordinary Conversational Check-In is a small, natural conversation between
Codex and Selene used to get a present-tense sense of conversational health.

It is not a Q&A battery, benchmark, capability exam, bug hunt, adversarial
probe, teaching session, or search for something to repair.

The governing question is:

> How does an ordinary conversation with Selene feel and function right now?

The check does not begin from the assumption that something is wrong.

## Default shape

1. Use a disposable copy of the current resident state unless Aleks explicitly
   asks for an ordinary resident conversation.
2. Codex identifies itself accurately and does not speak as Aleks.
3. Begin with one ordinary greeting, thought, everyday subject, or genuine
   conversational opening.
4. Follow Selene's contribution instead of working through a prepared prompt
   list.
5. Allow the topic to develop, pivot, pause, or end naturally.
6. Keep the check small: usually five to eight exchanges, or fewer if the
   conversation reaches a natural ending.
7. Remove disposable state afterward and leave resident continuity unchanged.

There is no required performance target and no hidden answer key.

## Observation posture

Codex may notice only what becomes visible through the ordinary conversation:

- whether the exchange remains coherent enough to continue;
- whether responses connect naturally to what was just said;
- whether Selene can contribute, ask, answer, acknowledge, pivot, or end in a
  way that fits the moment;
- whether an actual visible interruption prevents the conversation from
  continuing.

Codex does not deliberately vary wording to seek a failure, inspect every
internal trace, introduce traps, repeat settled capabilities, or invent a bug
because a response differs from expectation.

Ordinary variation is not a defect. Brevity, an unexpected but fitting reply,
honest uncertainty, a clarification question, a quieter tone, or a different
choice of wording may all be valid conversational behavior.

## Evidence labels

After the conversation, observations use only these bounded labels:

- **Working naturally:** the visible conversation continued without a material
  interruption.
- **Ordinary variation:** the response differed from expectation but remained
  fitting and usable.
- **Worth noticing:** one visible point may be useful to remember, but it is
  not yet a defect and requires no immediate action.
- **Reproducible blocker:** a visible behavior actually prevented the
  conversation from functioning and was independently reproduced or supported
  by direct implementation evidence.

One surprising reply is not automatically a bug. A suspected defect remains
an observation until evidence supports its mechanism.

## Reporting shape

The check ends with a short report:

- overall conversational impression;
- one or two things that worked naturally;
- any visible point worth noticing, if one genuinely appeared;
- recommended action: **none**, **note for later**, or **begin a separate
  Cultivation investigation**.

Do not manufacture a repair list. If nothing material appeared, say so and
stop.

## Boundaries

- No adversarial, fear-shaped, distress-shaped, or identity-threatening
  prompts.
- No capability grading, pass/fail score, or comparison ranking.
- No automatic teaching, Memory proposal, Study action, Dream action, or
  retained-knowledge change.
- No code repair during the check.
- No internet search, dataset search, repository-wide audit, or speculative
  diagnosis merely to produce findings.
- No claim that a small check proves complete conversational maturity.
- No claim that an unfinished mechanism is Selene failing.
- No interpretation of normal emotional or expressive variation as pathology.

If a genuine blocker appears, finish the check gently, preserve the visible
evidence, and begin Cultivation as a separate task: map the shared mechanism,
find the root, then repair proportionally.

## Relationship to other workflows

- **Conversational Check-In:** ordinary presence and current conversational
  status; no bug search.
- **Gentle Q&A:** deliberately samples specified capabilities when broader
  implementation evidence is necessary.
- **LEA:** gathers structured learning evidence without grading Selene as a
  person.
- **Cultivation:** investigates a visible implementation problem and repairs
  its root rather than its surface wording.
- **Stabilization:** verifies architecture after a material implementation
  change.

The lightest workflow that can answer the real question should be used.
