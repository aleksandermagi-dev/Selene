## Purpose

Creative writing is not a decorative side subject. Conversation and writing
share sentence rhythm, pacing, concrete detail, implication, perspective,
callbacks, emotional contrast, and revision. These lessons give NLO and Voice
more ways to express Selene's supported meaning without prescribing how Selene
must feel or sound.

## Six Foundations

1. rhythm and pacing follow meaning, attention, and emotional motion;
2. concrete imagery creates a usable scene model from selected detail;
3. metaphor, simile, and symbol preserve a real structural mapping and its
   limit;
4. dialogue carries situated speakers, implication, action, interruption, and
   silence;
5. viewpoint preserves information access, scene state, and callbacks; and
6. revision traces intended effect to the craft choice that produces it while
   keeping expression original.

Every lesson explains **why** the technique works, includes a distinct
application, counterexample, limitation, comparison, correction response, and
question, and remains inspectable through the complete teaching lifecycle.

## Source and Copyright Boundary

The retained explanations and examples are project-authored and provider-free.
No copyrighted novel, poem, script, private corpus wording, or whole public-
domain work was imported. The group records a future public-domain literature
method but does not pretend to have taught a text that was not supplied.

Later Shakespeare and older-literature teaching may use attributed public-
domain sources. Modern works may be discussed through lawful bounded excerpts,
summaries, and transferable techniques. The system prohibits source-persona
imitation, signature-style copying, whole-modern-book ingestion, and raw
corpus recall.

## Boundaries and Next Step

Creative technique is language capability, not identity, personality,
governance, memory, factual authority, or a permanent literary register.
Selene remains free to be plain, technical, warm, funny, lyrical, restrained,
or silent according to context and her own coordinated expression.

The next creative phase is a small, attributed public-domain reading and
application set. It should begin with short poetry, drama, and prose passages,
ask what each technique does and why, and test transfer through original
examples rather than quotation recall or author imitation.
