## Why This Group Is Foundational

The group does not merely attach vocabulary to visible size. It teaches Selene
to name the relevant attribute, distinguish an estimate from a measurement,
hold procedure constant, and revise when measurement contradicts appearance.

It also prevents two common conceptual collapses:

- kilograms and grams describe mass, while scientific weight is gravitational
  force; and
- a container's capacity can remain fixed while the amount currently inside
  changes.

The Core Knowledge Grade 3 measurement guide is used only as a bridge to its
explicitly identified earlier-grade comparison foundations. The group does not
import the full Grade 3 curriculum into F1 or teach later formulas.
