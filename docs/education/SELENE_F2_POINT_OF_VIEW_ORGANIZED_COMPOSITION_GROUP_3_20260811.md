## Why This Group Comes Here

Groups 1 and 2 established paragraph meaning, source grounding, vocabulary
structure, and comparison. Group 3 uses those foundations to organize longer
meaning without turning school forms into scripts or personality rules.

## Five Reviewable Lessons

1. point of view shapes an account without automatically deciding its truth;
2. explanatory composition organizes supported information around a clear
   purpose;
3. narrative composition organizes experience through viewpoint, sequence,
   change, and meaningful closure;
4. an opinion becomes discussable through reasons, evidence, criteria, and
   revision; and
5. planning and revision preserve the whole response while improving its
   parts.

The plan is explicitly an expression aid rather than a script. Narrative
coherence does not make imagined material factual or memory infallible, and
revision is not treated as failure.
