## What Is Implemented

### Language and Conversation Foundation

- Native Language Organ and supervised Selene Chat stabilization
- shared conversational intent routing
- grounded current self-state reporting
- dialogue fluency improvements
- structured semantic formation
- session-scoped dialogue workspace
- multi-part response obligations, conservative response-coverage checks, and
  one supported non-recursive completion pass
- current-session Conversation Spine landmarks plus Thread Loom branches,
  returns, dependencies, and final landings
- bounded implication, ellipsis, correction, and immediate reference handling
- bounded input detangling that preserves the raw message and does not guess
  ambiguous repairs

### Executable Ethical Testing Law

- least-impact testing is an executable status and review contract
- machinery, inspection, and synthetic checks are preferred
- stressful integrated testing requires demonstrated necessity
- missing behavior in an unfinished module is classified as an implementation
  observation, not Selene failing

The backend routes exist, but this law does not currently have a dedicated
Cocoon demonstration panel.

### Answer Engine Foundation

- route, evidence, answer, memory, and expression confidence remain separate
- answer-first obligations and graceful stopping are represented
- one bounded completion retry can complete missing obligations from approved
  knowledge or prompt-grounded method across ordinary answer routes
- open-ended comparison and planning can reason toward a best-current answer
  without requiring a known answer in advance
- exact arithmetic has checked results and an unsupported-operation path
- local-code inspection is limited to explicitly supplied or approved files,
  separates observation from interpretation, and cites inspected locations
- source-backed research is limited to attributed packets and distinguishes
  source statements, inference, disagreement, and missing evidence
- the Great Library remains an optional external resource rather than an
  internal authority

Verified math, source-backed research, and open-ended comparison/planning are
connected to supervised Selene Chat and pass through NLO/Voice without allowing
expression fluency to become correctness. Local-code inspection intentionally
remains limited to explicitly supplied or approved inputs and is not available
as autonomous Chat filesystem access. The adapters still do not perform broad
multi-domain synthesis and do not have a dedicated frontend workbench.

### Comprehension and Teaching

- accepted, review-only teaching packets can prepare source-bound understanding
  candidates
- teach-back, distinct application, limits, counterexample, correction
  readiness, and source alignment remain visible
- candidates can be evaluated, held, reopened, superseded, rejected, or
  explicitly approved
- Acquire, Integrate, and Express are persisted, inspectable stages
- revising an earlier stage invalidates dependent later-stage snapshots
- expression checks explanation, examples, analogies, questions, comparisons,
  conversational participation, and source parroting
- knowledge retention and supervised use require explicit Aleks approval
- memory, identity, personality, governance, training, and autonomy remain
  separate and unchanged
- the complete candidate and teaching-lifecycle workflow is visible in Cocoon

## Completion Gate

Selene is hackathon-ready when all of the following are true:

- the demonstrated capabilities run from clean public-safe inputs;
- the Answer Engine boundary is described honestly;
- Acquire, Integrate, Express, and Aleks-only approval are visibly inspectable;
- all memory, training, autonomy, identity, and governance guards remain false;
- the README distinguishes pre-existing Selene from event-window extensions;
- judges have a documented working access path;
- focused tests, direct synthetic smokes, frontend build, and final regression
  pass against the frozen demo;
- the video is public, under three minutes, audible, and free of private data;
- the Codex session evidence and final track are recorded;
- the submission is complete before the verified official deadline.

Nothing in this completion gate authorizes hidden retention, raw corpus recall,
identity or law mutation, model training, autonomy expansion, packaging, public
release, or expansion beyond bounded metacognitive observation.
