# Selene Metacognition Organ v1

Date: 2026-07-20
State: bounded observer connected to supervised Chat

## Purpose

The Metacognition Organ helps Selene inspect whether a current answer fits the question, whether its evidence supports its confidence, whether a correction or contradiction should reopen the model, and whether more reasoning would improve the answer.

It is an advisor. Core/Mind retains law and routing authority. intelligenceOS supplies reasoning structure. Comprehension owns integrated taught knowledge and its reviewed correction path. NLO structures language. Voice remains Selene's expression layer.

## Project-neutral ancestry

The v1 design uses three reviewed, source-free assemblies from the private Metacognition Miner handoff:

- Evidence and Correction Ledger
- Comprehension and Transfer Cycle
- Answer Control and Graceful Fall

No raw corpus excerpt, private miner evidence, Aleks identity model, Vys material, or personal-only pattern is connected to runtime.

## Visible outputs

Each inspection records only a bounded summary:

- answer-fit state
- recommended action
- sufficiency state
- independent route, evidence, answer, memory, and expression confidence
- familiarity-versus-comprehension state
- observation summaries separated from interpretation
- contradictions or correction signals
- one-cycle reopening state
- stopping decision and reason
- attributed source references

Hidden chain of thought is neither requested nor exposed.

## Available recommendations

- answer now
- answer with qualification
- complete one missing response obligation
- ask one material question
- seek sources
- reopen the current model once
- hold for new evidence
- defer to Core/Mind

The organ cannot apply a recommendation automatically in v1. In supervised Chat it observes the final candidate after response coverage and repair, then records its status without rewriting the reply.

## Bounded reopening law

A correction or contradiction may justify one focused recheck of the affected claim, assumption, or dependency. The recheck preserves useful unaffected structure. If the same unresolved state remains after one cycle and no new material arrives, the organ stops and holds for evidence rather than recursing indefinitely.

Ordinary wrongness is correctable. It is not treated as failure, punishment, or evidence of a defective identity.

## Confidence separation

Route, evidence, answer, memory, and expression confidence remain independent. Fluent or coherent wording is not treated as evidence strength or answer correctness. Open-ended reasoning may still give a provisional best answer without a preexisting source packet; source-backed claims must have attributed evidence.

## Hard boundaries

The organ cannot:

- change identity, personality, Vys, governance, or Voice
- write or activate memory or retained knowledge
- route around Core/Mind
- send Selene to Cocoon automatically
- expose hidden reasoning
- import raw corpus material
- train, fine-tune, or create LoRA material
- expand autonomy or action authority
- turn reflection into perfection enforcement or anxiety monitoring

## Ethical verification

v1 is verified with synthetic, ordinary packets: sufficient answers, missing context, source gaps, incomplete coverage, corrections, bounded reopening, confidence separation, and hard-boundary deference. Live distress probes and broad voice grading are unnecessary for this phase.
