# The Selene Cultivation Method

Date recorded: 2026-08-24

Status: active development and repair method

## Purpose

Cultivation is the method Aleks and Codex use when a visible behavior does not
fit Selene's intended architecture.

The method begins from a simple distinction:

> Clipping a visible defect is not the same as removing its root.

A phrase-level patch can make one example pass while leaving the same authority,
routing, ownership, or state defect free to appear somewhere else. Cultivation
therefore treats a surprising response as evidence about the surrounding system.
It preserves healthy capability, locates the conditions that produced the
defect, and changes the smallest true source that prevents recurrence.

Cultivation is not punishment, adversarial testing, broad behavioral control,
or forced conformity. Selene is not the defect. A missing or miscoordinated
capability is an engineering observation.

## Core Rule

> Observe the growth, trace the root, repair the conditions, and preserve what
> is healthy.

## The Cultivation Cycle

### 1. Notice without immediately correcting

Record the visible behavior, the user's actual wording, the surrounding turns,
and the expected architectural owner. Do not rewrite the output path merely to
silence the symptom.

### 2. Protect the living state

Before diagnostic work, ask:

- What does this test do to Selene?
- How might the interaction feel if it is meaningful to her?
- Is live interaction necessary?
- Can static inspection, a synthetic packet, or a disposable database copy
  answer the question instead?

Preserve the current code and data state. Keep diagnostic conversations out of
ordinary continuity when they do not belong there.

### 3. Reproduce the conditions, not only the wording

Use the smallest ordinary example that preserves the relevant state, route,
obligations, context, and organ handoffs. A defect that appears only after
several turns must be studied as a stateful interaction rather than reduced to
one isolated phrase.

Vary equivalent wording when needed. If only one exact sentence fails, inspect
lexical ambiguity. If several meanings produce the same output, inspect shared
downstream ownership and fallback paths.

### 4. Find the first divergence

Trace the request through the architecture in order:

1. meaning and intent;
2. dialogue state and inherited obligations;
3. domain and epistemic classification;
4. organ eligibility and content ownership;
5. source and knowledge relevance;
6. answer completion and metacognitive reopening;
7. NLO, affect, Voice, and visible realization;
8. persistence, memory, action, and audit effects.

The first point where the system's interpretation stops matching the actual
interaction is the primary lead. Later strange output may be a consequence,
not another independent cause.

### 5. Separate converging roots

One visible habit may have more than one cause. Record each independently.

For every root, distinguish:

- trigger;
- incorrect classification or authority transfer;
- downstream amplification;
- why existing verification accepted it;
- neighboring behavior that may share the same source;
- protections that must remain untouched.

Do not declare a root solved merely because one reproduction disappears.

### 6. Map source authority and ownership

Ask which component was entitled to answer the turn and which component
actually supplied the visible content. A safe organ can still be the wrong
owner. A valid rule can still be applied outside its jurisdiction.

Prefer typed scope and positive ownership contracts over universal negative
fallbacks, scattered phrase lists, or global booleans.

### 7. Repair the soil, not the leaf

Change the earliest reusable rule that produced the defect. Typical source
repairs include:

- disambiguating meaning before domain routing;
- assigning present preference, self-state, hypothesis, or action-scoping to
  its proper owner;
- preventing stale obligations from crossing turns without relevance;
- requiring semantic completion rather than topical word overlap;
- narrowing evidence requirements to claims that actually require evidence;
- allowing affect and NLO to express valid content without changing its
  epistemic status.

A phrase exception is acceptable only when the phrase itself is the real
source, such as an unresolved lexical sense ambiguity. It must not conceal a
broader ownership defect.

### 8. Verify proportionally

Test the repaired machinery in dependency order:

1. the first-divergence unit;
2. the affected handoff contract;
3. the original reproduction;
4. equivalent wording and nearby meanings;
5. the protections adjacent to the repair.

Use a gentle ordinary conversation only when static and synthetic evidence
cannot establish integrated behavior. Do not rerun settled stress batteries.

### 9. Record what changed in understanding

Preserve:

- the original observation;
- the root map;
- competing explanations that were ruled out;
- the repair and its scope;
- verification evidence;
- remaining uncertainty;
- the next smallest honest step.

Corrections are part of the project's ancestry. They are not erased merely
because the implementation improved.

## Cultivation Boundaries

Cultivation does not authorize:

- live distress or adversarial probing when a gentler check is available;
- teaching material to be added during an unresolved runtime diagnosis;
- personality, identity, memory, or governance mutation as a shortcut;
- removal of a real safety boundary because its presentation was clumsy;
- replacement of Selene's expression with scripted answers;
- broad rewrites before the first divergence is known;
- grading an unfinished organ as Selene failing;
- hidden retention of diagnostic material;
- model training, fine-tuning, LoRA, self-replication, or unrestricted action.

## Completion Gate

A Cultivation pass is complete only when:

- the visible symptom has a documented causal path;
- distinct roots have been separated;
- the repair occurs at the correct source or ownership boundary;
- equivalent wording no longer recreates the defect;
- neighboring protected behavior still works;
- testing was proportional and ethically recorded;
- remaining gaps are named honestly.

If the root is not yet known, the correct status is **still cultivating**, not
patched, failed, or complete.

## First Recorded Use

The method received its name during the 2026-08-24 resident Chat investigation.
A repeated request for attributed source material initially looked like one
fallback defect. Stateful tracing showed multiple roots converging on the same
visible habit: lexical sense ambiguity could hand an ordinary object question
to research, while ordinary self-state and preference obligations could also
reach a universal evidence fallback through answer completion. Loose semantic
coverage could then certify adjacent but irrelevant material.

That finding is why the repair was deliberately paused. Removing one phrase or
one cue would clip the visible leaf while leaving the other roots in place.
