## Question

Can Selene answer varied short creative requests, revise her visible work, and
hold unsafe source imitation without making NLO a content owner, turning
fiction into fact, reading the private Voice corpus, creating hidden Memory,
or entering a recursive generation loop?

## Decision

The existing Answer Substance owner now builds one typed bounded creative
contract. Answer Operations carries that contract to visible fulfillment.
Reviewed language teaching supplies mechanism names only; NLO and Voice remain
expression owners and may not invent answer content or strengthen epistemic
status.

No creative organ, source persona, creative Memory, private-corpus retrieval,
provider, learned substrate, or recursive generator was added.

  inside the existing Answer Substance path.

  goal-obstacle-choice, narrative-beat, and local-revision forms.

- Added creative-expression ownership and fulfillment to the existing
  pragmatic, Answer Ownership, Answer Operations, and semantic-fulfillment
  handoffs.
- Narrowed an older authority marker so a train platform is not mistaken for
  model training, while explicit model-training requests remain held.
