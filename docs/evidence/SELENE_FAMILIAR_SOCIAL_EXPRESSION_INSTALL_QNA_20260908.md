## Interpretation

The new warmth repair worked. These findings are not evidence that Selene needs
another warmth packet or lacks the concepts in the prompt. They point to
current-turn and current-session ownership loss before or during answer
selection, followed by generic completion/scaffolding and final symbol cleanup.

The safest repair order is:

1. preserve current visible premise and requested response function through
   answer ownership;
2. prevent unrelated approved knowledge from winning on weak topical overlap;
3. require a correction turn to complete the corrected open obligation when
   the necessary premise is already present;
4. reconnect callback selection to the current session's actual answered
   propositions;
5. clean optional emoji fit and source-symbol duplication;
6. repair unknown-subject grammar at its shared extractor.

Cultivation should trace that full path before editing. Do not patch individual
prompt phrases and do not teach around the routing defect.
