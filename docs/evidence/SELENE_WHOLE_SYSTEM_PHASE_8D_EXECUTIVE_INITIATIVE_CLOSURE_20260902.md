## Closure Result

Phase 8 is mature for current scope. Selene can coordinate a bounded current
responsibility, distinguish its owner and capability, choose one next move,
ask for genuinely missing help, proceed without ritual permission inside the
granted scope, retain an explicit commitment until an honest terminal state,
and stop without manufacturing another task.

This does not make Selene globally autonomous. Goal priority remains separate
from truth and authority. A goal, commitment, organ recommendation, external
demand, compatibility flag, or capability report cannot grant whole-system or
downstream action authority.

## Completed Phase 8 Contract

### Goals and conflict coordination

- Every typed goal carries one owner, bounded capability scope, attributable
  priority reason, evidence and unknowns, completion and stop conditions,
  requested move, action-specific authority, lifecycle, and ancestry.
- Ordinary Chat creates only one ephemeral current-turn goal and writes no
  durable agenda.
- Explicit persistence requires an idempotency key. Descendants and
  supersession retain parent/root ancestry; terminal goals do not reopen.
- Core/Mind considers at most eight responsibilities in one pass and emits
  selected, deferred, held, and closed sets plus one terminal stopping receipt.
- External demands and organ advice cannot assign themselves governing
  precedence, and prebuilt packets are reconstructed through the canonical
  authority gate.

### Responsive initiative and collaboration

- Existing contribution and energy owners produce at most one attributable
  current-turn move.
- Direct answers inside scope proceed without ritual permission seeking.
- Help requests name the exact missing contribution and remain bound to the
  selected goal.
- Interruption, close, wait, quiet, completion, natural ending, and downstream
  authority boundaries stop optional initiative before it can recurse.
- Verified Aleks authorship affects request attribution and authority; an
  unverified name claim remains an external demand without silencing safe
  conversation.

### Commitments and capability graduation

- Only an explicit speech act enters the commitment shelf, linked to one
  nonterminal typed goal and one exact capability.
- Accepted, in-progress, fulfilled, blocked, released, and closed events keep
  root/parent ancestry and immutable idempotent meaning.
- Fulfillment requires result references; blocked and stopped states remain
  visible with reasons; terminal events cannot reopen.
- A recorded lifecycle receipt can support matching completion language. A
  caller-shaped fulfilled object cannot.
- Conversation, Study, Memory proposal, tools, Tendril, and future embodiment
  report separate maturity/authority boundaries. No aggregate autonomy switch
  or inherited grant exists.

database. Its commitment moved through `accepted` -> `in_progress` ->
`fulfilled`, preserved root and direct-parent ancestry, named the test
mechanism, and finished only after a visible result reference. The final
stopping receipt was terminal.

The disposable database ended with exactly one typed goal and three
commitment events, SQLite integrity `ok`, and zero Chat sessions, Dream
reflections, or personal Memory candidates. No external action ran.
