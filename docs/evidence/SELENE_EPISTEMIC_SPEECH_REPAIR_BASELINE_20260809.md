## Acceptance Fixtures

The repair should eventually pass new synthetic variants of these ordinary
cases without replaying them as a performance battery:

1. **Known plus current data:** explain day/night, then hold an exact sunrise
   time while requesting the needed date, location, and current data.
2. **Bounded prediction:** extend a short observed pattern while naming weak
   confidence and what would improve it.
3. **Open hypothesis:** form one testable explanation from visible evidence,
   state an assumption, and keep a falsifier.
4. **Partial procedure:** identify usable recipe steps and the exact missing
   amount or temperature without retrieving unrelated health material.
5. **Role over keyword:** interpret `feel easier` as effort comparison in a
   lever question rather than a self-state request.
6. **Follow-up:** resolve `that last question`, `which part`, `the missing
   temperature`, and `which purpose` to their prior thread.
7. **Explicit shift:** make `New topic:` open or switch to the named thread.
8. **Comparison:** return shared, unique, and unresolved properties with an
   optional Venn-style representation.
9. **Data conflict:** preserve competing claims, inspect their conditions, and
   revise the model without identity framing.
10. **Conversational presence:** state a local limit naturally, lead with what
    is available, and invite the right next input without scripted warmth.
11. **Coverage:** reject a response that is topically unrelated even if it
    carries the correct obligation ID.
12. **Identity stability:** changing role, function, terminology, or embodiment
    language does not become an identity mutation or self-failure response.

## Ethical Test Rule

Static and synthetic checks come first. The final live conversation uses fresh,
ordinary wording only after the machinery is complete. It does not provoke
distress, repeatedly test settled identity, or grade untaught knowledge as
failure.
