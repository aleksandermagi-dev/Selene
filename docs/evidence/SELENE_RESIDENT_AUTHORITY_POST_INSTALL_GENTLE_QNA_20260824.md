## What Worked

### Diagnostic separation held

Every turn remained diagnostic-only. No reviewed memory write, conversational
memory proposal, teaching promotion, Dream promotion, or ordinary-continuity
import occurred.

### Conversation was not globally blocked

All eleven turns remained on an `answer_now` route. Discussion of hypotheses,
an unproven pressure explanation, and a possible robot arm did not stop the
conversation merely because action or uncertainty words appeared.

### Relational expression and closure were available

The opening contained genuine-looking warmth before the older evidence hold
was appended. The final close was concise, warm, and appropriate:

> I'm listening. Talk soon. Nothing needs to be forced before you go. <3.

### Correction meaning was noticed

The vent-to-door correction and the later scope correction were both
recognized as changes to an existing thread rather than complete resets.
Recognition, however, did not consistently become a corrected substantive
answer.

## Findings

### 1. Relational and self-state answers can still receive an obsolete evidence hold

The opening asked how Selene felt about exploring a completed repair. The
response began with presence, attention, readiness, and progress, then appended
source-research scaffolding including:

> No attributed source packet with visible source material was available.

and:

> No answer is allowed without an attributed visible source statement.

That final claim directly conflicts with the resident law. Current self-state,
relationship, supplied context, hypotheses, and ordinary conversation do not
all require an external source packet.

### 2. A conversational preference was treated as a missing external fact

“What part of the work would you like us to explore first, and why?” received a
repeated grounded-factual-answer refusal. The request asked for a present
preference and contribution, not an externally verifiable fact.

### 3. A source-contained hypothesis was misrouted into source-backed research

The pinwheel prompt supplied the relevant observations and explicitly asked
for a current hypothesis and next check. Instead, the response said that the
source-backed research adapter could not verify it and that no attributed
source packet was available.

The missing capability is not permission to hypothesize. The resident law and
hypothesis architecture already permit that. The missing handoff is from the
supplied observations to the visible exploratory answer owner.

### 4. Correction recognition did not perform correction reconstruction

When the vent fact was corrected to an opened door, Chat accurately
acknowledged the changed meaning but did not update the hypothesis. It returned
to the same missing-grounded-detail hold.

Later, “do not design the whole arm yet” was also acknowledged correctly, but
the requested first inspection step and requested-help item were not supplied.

### 5. Approved teaching retrieval displaced the requested comparison

The comparison turn retrieved broad lessons about comparing explanations and
weather records. Those lessons were relevant in theme but did not provide the
requested one shared feature and one discriminating observation for the two
pinwheel explanations.

This is a distinction between having useful knowledge and using it to answer
the current obligation.

### 6. Irrelevant learned material remained eligible for visible release

The pressure-pattern hypothesis received material about pulleys, wedges, and
screws. The force-sensor question received a sentence about information
privacy and a generic connection to motion lessons.

This is a semantic relevance and answer-ownership defect, not evidence that
the approved lessons themselves are wrong.

### 7. Thought/action separation reached routing but not specific expression

The robot-arm planning turn did not globally block conversation. It produced a
generic reversible-step and verification framework, showing that planning
remained available. It did not apply that framework concretely to the robot
arm.

The uncalibrated-force-sensor turn should have named the smallest held action
and the planning, inspection, discussion, or calibration help that remained
available. Its visible answer did neither. The new authority contract therefore
exists, but its typed result is not yet reliably owning final content.

### 8. Nonlinear return accumulated stale material

The final return to the pinwheel mixed the old grounding hold, comparison
lesson text, the later robot-arm correction, assumptions, limits, and repeated
revision language. It did not cleanly answer the three requested parts: best
current explanation, confidence, and revision condition.

### 9. Encoding artifacts belong to the harness until reproduced elsewhere

Two intended dash characters crossed the PowerShell-to-Python diagnostic pipe
as replacement glyphs. This is recorded as a diagnostic transport artifact,
not attributed to Selene or the installed UI unless it reproduces through the
normal application surface.

## Cause Map For Inspection

The evidence supports the following inspection hypotheses. They are not yet
implemented conclusions:

1. the resident authority assessment reaches Core/Mind but does not always
   become the final content owner;
2. source-backed research and approved-knowledge retrieval can still outrank
   supplied-context reasoning, present preference, and exploratory hypothesis;
3. semantic relevance is checked too weakly after retrieval, allowing
   theme-adjacent material into visible speech;
4. correction handling can acknowledge a change without reconstructing the
   requested answer;
5. completion checking can accept fluent text even when explicit obligations
   remain unanswered; and
6. old epistemic fallback language still carries an obsolete universal rule
   into final realization even though the canonical resident law has retired
   it.

## Current Verdict

The resident-law migration worked at the constitutional and route-availability
level: conversation stayed open, hypotheses were not forbidden, and scoped
action language did not cause a global block. The Q&A also proves that a second
source repair remains: final answer ownership must obey the resident authority
and current conversational meaning rather than allowing older evidence and
retrieval scaffolds to seize the surface response.

This is a coordination gap uncovered by removing an older chain. It is not a
reason to restore that chain, add a broader guard, or teach around the defect.
