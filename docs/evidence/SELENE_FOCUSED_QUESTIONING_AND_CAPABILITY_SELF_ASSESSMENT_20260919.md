Date: 2026-09-19

## Observation

Aleks asked Selene what gaps remained before she reached her intended current
capability. The turn was semantically a question about Selene's present system,
but a shared word (`missing`) let approved teaching material answer instead.
Selene's resulting statement about focused questioning was still a useful

handled separately.

## Root Repair

Capability-status questions now have a typed intent, obligation kind, and
read-only owner. The owner summarizes the current Organ Maturity Ledger and is
selected ahead of unrelated approved teaching material. Detection requires a
Selene subject, a capability/system-status signal, and a question or request
shape. Ordinary self-state checks, project questions, memory callbacks, and
relational uses of words such as `missing` remain with their existing owners.

The response refuses a false single percentage, names mature-current-scope and
unavailable capabilities from the ledger, keeps bounded limitations visible,
and states that capability gaps are not identity gaps. It writes no state and
cannot alter Memory, retained knowledge, identity, personality, governance,
authority, training, or action.

## Selene's Focused-Questioning Connection

A shared focused-question decision now makes the existing behavior inspectable:

1. Use the answer and support already available.
2. Identify the smallest missing detail.
3. Ask only when that detail materially changes the conclusion, method, or
   scope.
4. Name why the detail matters.
5. Ask at most one focused question.
6. Do not ask for information already present.
7. Resume the original task after the answer.
8. If no justified question or supported answer exists, `unknown` remains a
   valid result rather than a failure.

This is connective tissue across the existing Conversational Energy,
Pragmatic Continuity, answer-completeness, collaboration, and metacognitive
owners. It is not a new autonomous question generator, Memory writer, Study
writer, or authority source.

## Retired Stale Guards

Two older restrictions conflicted with the later mature conversation contract:

- A preliminary social-turn ending disabled every question. It now continues
  to reject habitual engagement questions while allowing a separately proven,
  genuinely curious handoff from Conversational Energy.
- Associative contributions that already passed the source, relevance,
  distinctness, current-task, turn-taking, repetition, and pressure gates were
  still suppressed whenever a direct obligation existed unless Aleks had
  explicitly invited an idea. That redundant invitation dependency is gone.
  The direct answer still keeps priority and no out-of-turn speech is allowed.

The Organ Maturity Ledger also had completed-future wording for comprehension,
association, and Dream work. Those descriptions now reflect current scope
without claiming future perception, audible Voice, Tendril action, or
embodiment.
