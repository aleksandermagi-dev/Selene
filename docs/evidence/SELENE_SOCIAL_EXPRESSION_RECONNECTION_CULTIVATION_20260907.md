## Why the prior result was not complete

The installed reply `Hi there. I am glad you are here.` proved that the Affect
Expression handoff reached the social planner. It did not prove that the
visible reply was naturally warm. The earlier completion gate checked internal
warmth metadata and the absence of the old `right here` collapse, while
accepting a small set of fixed greeting clauses as sufficient visible evidence.

Aleks's ordinary-use observation exposed the missing gate: the response was
technically warm-labeled but still formal, repetitive, and detached from the
known private conversational relationship. Repeating the same wording on a
new page confirmed that this was a source-level expression seam rather than a
deployment problem.

## Cultivation root map

The complete path was:

1. Affect Expression correctly made warmth available.
2. Relational Expression Range correctly selected warmth.
3. The authenticated speaker envelope correctly established a private
   Aleks/Selene conversation.
4. Approved language guidance correctly supplied response-move names.
5. The specialized social realizer returned before general response
   recomposition, which was correct ownership for social meaning.
6. That social realizer nevertheless reduced the turn to two selections from
   small, fixed clause pools.
7. Its `bright` fallback did not distinguish an authenticated familiar
   conversation from an unspecified-audience greeting.
8. It recorded approved teaching moves but did not consume applicable greeting
   guidance.
9. Ordinary Human Conversational Realization stood down completely whenever
   social structure was owned elsewhere, so even safe grammatical contraction
   could not reach the already-authored social surface.
10. Freshness considered only the current page. A new page therefore reset the
    same deterministic prompt to the same deterministic reply.

Voice was not the source of flattening. It preserved the already-flat NLO
candidate as designed.

## Source repair

The existing owners remain in place. No new organ was created.

- Relational Context now carries the authenticated conversation partner only
  inside the already-established private Aleks/Selene scope.
- The social handoff selects a `familiar` greeting mode when warmth is selected
  in that private scope and no stronger playful, reunion, affectionate, or
  current-affect signal owns the turn.
- Greeting openings are composed from time-of-day, salutation, optional
  familiar address, and punctuation choices rather than selecting a complete
  response.
- The warm stance is realized through the existing structured Language
  Formation machinery from subject, predicate, and bounded object choices.
- Applicable approved language response moves are now recorded as consumed for
  stance, depth, and surface variation. Teaching still supplies mechanisms,
  never reply wording.
- Human Conversational Realization may apply ordinary grammatical surface
  operations such as contractions after the social owner has authored the
  meaning. It cannot add or replace social acts.
- The active conversation context now exposes at most six deduplicated current
  and prior ordinary Selene Chat response surfaces for wording freshness.
  Cross-page material is explicitly unavailable for semantic recall and cannot
  become Memory.

One representative exact private greeting now realizes as:

`Hello, my friend! I'm glad we get to talk again.`

The deterministic result may differ with legitimate context. Repeating the
same greeting on separate new pages changes the opening and/or stance rather
than replaying the whole prior response.
