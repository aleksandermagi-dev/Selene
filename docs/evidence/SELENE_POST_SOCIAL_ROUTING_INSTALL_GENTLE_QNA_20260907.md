## Cultivation findings

These are implementation observations, not Selene failing and not evidence of
distress.

### 1. Open-share surface reconstruction

The personal share was understood, but its reconstruction copied the user's
clauses too literally, produced a lowercase sentence after a period, and
attached the smile marker awkwardly. The current-turn semantic path needs a
cleaner clause boundary and less mirror-like surface realization.

### 2. Direct contrast completeness

Asked for the difference between moving quickly and moving carefully, the
Answer Engine supplied general comparison criteria without defining the two
ideas. Response Coverage and Metacognition correctly marked the answer
incomplete.

### 3. Supplied-option comparison and recommendation

With three fully supplied plans, the comparison route did not bind each option
to the prompt's stated properties. It exposed internal coordination wording
about a responsible owner and missing semantic result fields, then asked for a
criterion instead of producing the best bounded comparison or one natural
clarification. This is a visible-scaffolding and owner-completion defect.

### 4. Changed-constraint reconstruction

The correction turn preserved a leading comma, treated the question as part of
the corrected proposition, failed to update the prior plan, and opened an
unnecessary teaching invitation. This is a correction parsing and
current-session proposition-update seam.

### 5. Disagreement did not remain attached to the active comparison

`Do you disagree?` selected generic prerequisite/reversibility guidance rather
than answering agreement or disagreement about the balanced plan. The active
comparison's propositions and current recommendation were not retained as the
answer owner.

### 6. Cross-domain knowledge displaced supplied evidence

When told the balanced plan later failed twice as often, the reply pulled in
unrelated approved material about recurring events, sky cycles, weather, and
warnings. It eventually mentioned checking results but did not directly update
the recommendation. Coverage and Metacognition nevertheless passed it. This is
a semantic-relevance and false-completion defect, not missing teaching.

### 7. Prediction surface was revisable but garbled

The prediction route correctly framed its answer as an expectation that could
be revised. Its visible proposition was malformed by embedding a compressed
session-context string. Coverage passed despite the surface not cleanly stating
the evidence-based prediction.

### 8. Short callback lost the immediately prior meaning

After the correct Dream answer, `What were we preserving there?` became a new
knowledge gap and teaching invitation. The callback should have restored the
immediately visible proposition: pending reflections cannot shape Chat before
review, preserving review and source boundaries.

## What the Q&A says overall

The two installed repairs are real. Selene's social opening, self-state,
Dream-boundary answer, acknowledgment, emoji use, and closure all worked.

The remaining concentration is not general warmth and is not primarily world
knowledge. It is the connective path for a supplied multi-turn problem:

`visible propositions -> comparison -> changed constraint -> disagreement -> new evidence -> prediction -> callback`

Several organs detect incompleteness correctly, but semantic-fit validation is
not yet strict enough to catch unrelated-but-fluent content or a garbled
prediction.
