## Typed Source Contract

New and revised teaching candidates retain only the applicable roles from this
bounded vocabulary:

- `source_statement`;
- `inference`;
- `example`;
- `practice`;
- `verification`; and
- `current_fact`.

Each role names the instructional content fields it governs and the attributed
source references supporting that role. The receipt explicitly states that a
source statement is not automatic truth and that inference remains distinct
from source statement.

All 152 implemented curriculum lessons across 26 groups now reconstruct one
shared five-role lesson contract: source statement, inference, example,
practice, and verification. None is labeled `current_fact`. The language-
capability teaching shelf and disposable public-safe demonstration use the
same shared lifecycle contract without becoming answer-bearing curriculum.

## Instructional-Why Contract

Integrate cannot complete new or revised work unless one reviewable receipt
contains:

- a bounded why kind;
- the explanatory relationship;
- why the relationship matters;
- its scope;
- a failure or exception condition; and
- unresolved uncertainty.

The receipt remains instructional only. It does not enter or extend the
affective Why + Salience helper, claim an inner state, prescribe expression,
or change factual certainty. Acquire, Integrate, and Express continue to
preserve vocabulary, near concepts, examples, counterexamples, limits,
questions, comparisons, original-language reconstruction, correction
readiness, and the source-parroting check.

`developing`, `needs_representation`, `needs_prerequisite`, `revisit`, and
`unclear` are visible non-forced learning states. A stage snapshot records the
state and explicitly records that completion was not forced; these states are
not grades, diagnoses, speed targets, or worth judgments.

## Durable Knowledge and Current Claims

The proposal payload, lifecycle snapshots, approval receipt, and retrieval
result now carry typed knowledge and freshness classes.

A `current_fact` role must use the matching time-sensitive knowledge and
freshness classes. It cannot inherit standing curriculum authorization. Even
after explicit item review, it is retained as a reviewed current claim with
`requires_fresh_current_source`, not activated as durable ordinary-Chat
knowledge. Durable public-academic foundations continue through the existing
reviewed knowledge path.

Open candidates can receive the Phase 6B contract idempotently when they are
re-proposed for new or revised work. Already approved concepts are not
rewritten by this migration behavior.
