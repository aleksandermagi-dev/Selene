# Selene Conversation Cultivation Phase 8 — Shared Realization Cleanup

Date: 2026-09-12  
Status: implemented and proportionally verified

## Question

Can a completed correction reach visible speech without exposing internal
labels, repeating generic recovery prose, damaging punctuation, or allowing a
stale learning-gap fallback to replace newly supported knowledge?

## Root findings

The remaining correction defect was not a failure to understand the changed
premise. Current-session state had already consumed the revision and produced
the updated answer. The social realization path independently narrated the
same correction again as `corrected meaning` and added a generic account of
preserved context. Its fallback correction extractor also retained the comma
after `Actually,` and absorbed the following `What changes?` question. One
proved owner result therefore became two competing visible explanations, one
of them malformed.

The focused neighboring matrix then exposed a second shared-release defect.
Conversationally taught facts were retained and retrievable, but ordinary
property and meaning questions such as `What color is the sky?` and `What does
trailstar mean?` were not recognized as explicit subject requests. Semantic
Relevance consequently held the valid approved resource as peripheral and the
older learning-gap invitation won release. This was stale fallback
contamination, not failed teaching or retention.

## Source repair

Selene Chat now passes the existing typed session-revision completion receipt
into NLO and its social-act plan. When that receipt proves the responsible
content owner has recomputed the exact correction, the social layer retains
only its acknowledgement role. It does not restate the changed meaning or
invent another description of what remained valid. Standalone corrections
without a proved content owner still realize their supplied changed meaning
directly, without exposing an internal correction label.

The shared correction extractor now removes marker punctuation after common
correction introductions and separates a following question at a sentence
boundary. The visible content owner therefore receives clean meaning rather
than a leading comma or an attached request.

Semantic Relevance now recognizes ordinary property and named-meaning
questions. A single overlap term is still insufficient by itself: the term
must be grammatically requested and must occur in the leading subject position
of the reviewed title or central claim, while entity, operation, and requested-
function alignment continue to pass independently. Function words such as
`mean` and `meaning` no longer inflate the requested subject. This restores
freshly taught knowledge without weakening the existing protections against
nearby one-word lesson collisions.

## Evidence

The bounded Phase 8 matrix passed 88 checks across Semantic Relevance,
Conversational Teaching, Social Language Realization, NLO, Human

- a reviewed claim directly about a requested property is eligible;
- a reviewed definition directly about a named term is eligible;
- freshly taught sky and trailstar knowledge is recalled without another
  teaching invitation; and
- unrelated one-word lesson collisions remain held.
