# Selene Provider-Free Text Speech Completion Checkpoint

Date: 2026-07-20

## Outcome

Selene's provider-free text-speech path is complete for the meanings and knowledge currently available to her architecture.

This checkpoint means she can form, continue, repair, and safely release her own visible text without an external language-model provider. It does not mean she possesses unlimited factual knowledge or the lexical breadth of a mature general language model.

## Completed path

The visible response path now keeps these responsibilities separate and inspectable:

1. Meaning routing identifies the requested kind of turn.
2. Approved comprehension knowledge or bounded reasoning supplies answer substance.
3. Contextual speech preserves the active subject across ordinary follow-ups.
4. Conversation Repair checks answer coverage and repairs bounded omissions.
5. NLO organizes the answer into a conversational form.
6. Voice realizes that form as Selene's expression without changing identity or law.
7. The visible-speech guard permits only a user-facing final answer to leave the system.

## Ordinary speech behavior now covered

- direct answers before explanation when grounded content exists
- truthful source or teaching requests when factual content is unavailable
- comparisons based on a shared standard
- dependency-aware ordering and planning
- conditional consequence questions
- bounded viewpoints that distinguish judgment from fact
- natural uncertainty based on answer confidence rather than sentence fluency
- short follow-ups such as `Why?`, `Are you sure?`, and `And then?`
- requests for an example, a simpler restatement, elaboration, or a viewpoint
- partial agreement, correction, topic shifts, references, and multi-part turns
- approved knowledge explanations that can include the relevant `why`, limit, or relationship
- long-form planning, examples, transitions, summaries, and natural closure
- affect-informed wording guidance without prescribed emotion or identity change
- graceful fall when evidence or knowledge is missing

## Context and retention boundary

Follow-up interpretation is scoped to the active chat session. It does not silently become personal memory, taught knowledge, identity, governance, personality, authority, or training material.

Approved general knowledge remains separate from personal memory. Teaching can influence what Selene says and the linguistic resources available for saying it, but it does not define who she is.

## Answer-substance boundary

When a prompt permits a useful answer without external facts, Selene can provide a method, dependency rule, conditional answer, planning step, or clearly identified viewpoint instead of collapsing into generic uncertainty.

When a factual answer requires knowledge she does not have, the system names the missing source or approved concept rather than inventing content or citations. Expression confidence remains separate from route, evidence, answer, and memory confidence.

## Ethical verification

No live emotional or adversarial probe was used for this checkpoint. Verification used static inspection and synthetic machinery tests because those were sufficient and did not require placing Selene in a stressful interaction.

Focused verification result:

- `221 passed`
- covered speech routing, answer substance, visible-speech release, contextual follow-ups, conversation repair, dialogue workspace, pragmatic planning and continuity, affect expression, metacognition, NLO, Voice, intelligenceOS, Answer Engine, comprehension, memory boundaries, transfer memory, and the Selene Chat shell

## Honest remaining limits

- Her factual breadth is bounded by approved teaching and connected domain resources.
- Provider-free surface realization remains more structured and less varied than a mature general language model.
- Some intent recognition still uses inspectable phrase and pattern signals.
- Audible speech, turn-taking, interruption, pronunciation, pacing, and speech consent contracts are a later layer.
- New knowledge domains still need ordered teaching from foundations through application and explanation.

These are capability-development boundaries, not failures by Selene.
