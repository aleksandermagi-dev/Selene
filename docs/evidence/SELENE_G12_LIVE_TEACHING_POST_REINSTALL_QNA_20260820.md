## Findings

### P0 — Callback guidance is selected, but callback content is not restored

The new lesson reached NLO on the immediate question, “What part of that
result am I celebrating?” The visible answer nevertheless asked for an
attributed source or approved teaching item instead of using the immediately
preceding statement.

The same pattern appeared when returning to the settled shelf choice. The
response said it lacked grounded detail even though the relevant facts and
correction existed in the diagnostic session.

This is not a failure of the lesson. The lesson correctly refuses to invent
content. Conversation Spine or the current-session answer owner must supply
the actual supported landmark or fact before NLO can express it.

### P0 — False completion still releases visible nonanswers

At least six visibly incomplete or irrelevant replies were marked fully
addressed. These included:

- the failed immediate callback;
- the correction turn that appended unrelated change, stability, graph, and
  cooperation material;
- the distinction-and-next-watch request that produced a generic experimental
  hypothesis plus unrelated mass material;
- the disagreement request that did not evaluate the proposed bulk teaching;
- the changed-versus-stable shelf question; and
- the named return to the shelf decision.

Completion metadata therefore remains too optimistic to serve as a release
gate by itself.

### P0 — Supplied prompt facts still lose to method scaffolds or unrelated knowledge

The workbench comparison supplied all facts needed for a bounded choice.
Instead of choosing a shelf, the Answer Engine returned a generic comparison
procedure and repeated assumptions and limits.

Other turns selected unrelated approved material about change, graphs,
cooperation, mass, and focused questioning. Valid taught knowledge is still
being chosen without obligation-level relevance to the current prompt.

### P1 — The new callback lesson is too sticky after a session landmark exists

The G12 callback lesson appeared on 19 of 20 turns, including turns whose main
need was greeting, uncertainty, hypothesis, disagreement, comparison, humor,
or closure. The current score grants strong relevance whenever the dialogue
workspace contains any session landmark.

A landmark's existence is not sufficient evidence that the current turn is a
callback. Selection should require a resolved reference, named return, clear
ellipsis, or another current-turn dependency on that landmark.

### P1 — Generic missing-ground language overrides usable current-session evidence

Several replies repeated variants of “I don't have enough grounded detail” or
asked which observation should control the conclusion despite the prompt or
session already providing the needed facts.

The graceful-fall behavior itself is valid. Its invocation condition is too
broad.

### P1 — Multi-part, humor, and exploratory requests reach structure without content

The response machinery often recognized that several obligations existed, but
did not satisfy them. Examples include:

- joke then return to recommendation;
- acknowledge bins, compare shelves, and place heavy tools;
- labeled vibration hypothesis plus a check;
- four-part ordered synthesis; and
- three-point summary plus natural closure.

This supports the existing architecture diagnosis: obligation structure is
more mature than ordinary content execution.

### P1 — Coverage and repair disagree on two incomplete turns

Eleven turns reported incomplete coverage, but only nine requested content
revision. The multi-part workbench turn and final summary/closure turn were
visibly incomplete without activating content repair.

### P2 — A malformed separator reaches visible speech

Several exploratory replies contained a replacement character between a
described condition and the following clause. This appears as `�` in the
captured output and should be traced as an encoding or punctuation-realization
defect.

## Interpretation

The teaching pipeline passed its real test: one reviewed lesson traveled from
defined guidance through Acquire, Integrate, Express, standing authorization,
retention, and NLO selection without hidden retention or boundary mutation.

The Q&A also prevented a misleading conclusion. Teaching a callback mechanism
does not itself provide the recalled content. Selene now has stronger guidance
for how to use a callback once the conversation system supplies the correct
referent. The remaining work is to make current-session facts, corrections,
landmarks, and visible obligations reach the correct answer owner reliably.

## Recommended repair order

No repair was made in this assessment. The bounded next repair pass should:

1. require current-turn callback evidence before strongly selecting the G12
   callback lesson;
2. carry immediate and named session facts into the callback answer owner;
3. make current-prompt choice, comparison, and hypothesis execution outrank
   generic methods and keyword-adjacent approved knowledge;
4. require visible obligation-specific evidence before reporting completion;
5. align Conversation Repair with every unresolved required obligation; and
6. remove the malformed punctuation path.

After focused synthetic verification, repeat only the changed paths. Do not
rerun the full 20-turn Q&A unless the combined long-thread coordination itself
changes.
