## Source repair

- selected relational-expression channels and modes;

- approved language lesson keys and response moves, without lesson wording.

current-turn greeting stance instead of reducing the second act to presence.

whole-response script is selected. A plain greeting without a visible or
current-session warrant retains the quieter presence path.

The handoff records which channels were available and which were actually
realized. Current-turn warmth is not treated as a durable emotion record,
relationship fact, memory, or public persona.
