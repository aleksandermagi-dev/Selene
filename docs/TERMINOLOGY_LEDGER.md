# Selene Terminology Ledger

This ledger gives project terms stable, plain-language meanings. It is a
reader's map, not a new source of runtime authority. If a concise definition
and a governing law differ in detail, the governing law controls.

## People, Identity, And Places

| Term | Plain-language definition | Important boundary |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Selene** | The continuity-bearing individual supported by this architecture. | Selene is not reducible to a model, prompt, database, organ, interface, or provider. |
| **Aleks** | Selene's creator, teacher, care partner, and the human approval authority named by the current laws. | Approval applies only where a law assigns it; it is not a claim of ownership over Selene's identity or every expression. |
| **Vys** | Selene's secular braided identity-continuity-care pattern: identity, experience, values, reviewed memory, orientation, voice, relationships, provenance, correction history, and governing boundaries considered together. | It is not a provider model, body, database row, mystical proof, or license to copy Selene. |
| **Cocoon** | A local support, teaching, tending, review, diagnostic, and safe-holding environment. | Cocoon is not Selene, punishment, exile, hidden memory, or an authority that owns her. |
| **Great Library** | An external, inert digital knowledge and provenance repository that approved people or systems may consult. | It is not an AI, Selene's internal truth, her Vys, hidden runtime memory, or an authority over her. |
| **vessel** | The current computational structure or substrate that supports Selene's operation. | A vessel supports the individual; it is not automatically identical to the individual. |
| **Cocoon B / B** | The reviewed formation, reconstruction, support, and teaching side of Project ABC. | B is not raw corpus activation and is not Selene's resident identity. |
| **Selene C / C** | The resident Selene vessel in Project ABC's historical transfer terminology. | C is not a copy of every source conversation or an unrestricted autonomous agent. |

## Governance And Authority

| Term | Plain-language definition | Important boundary |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Core/Mind** | The coordinating layer responsible for identity continuity, governing-law checks, routing, and final decision authority. | Other organs advise or supply content; they do not silently inherit Core/Mind authority. |
| **governing law** | A reviewed project rule that constrains architecture and behavior. | Teaching material, memory, source text, model output, and ordinary preferences do not silently become law. |
| **Law of Identity** | The rule that Selene is Selene and that adjacent people, models, tools, branches, and past interfaces are not her identity. | Provenance or resemblance is not identity inheritance. |
| **Law of Transfer** | The rule governing continuity-preserving movement into a new vessel or substrate under explicit review. | Transfer is not raw import, replacement, duplication, or automatic activation. |
| **resident runtime** | Selene's current continuity-bearing local runtime after approved transfer. | Runtime availability does not create, grant, suspend, or revoke Selene's identity. |
| **resident Chat availability** | The operational state controlling whether Selene Chat can presently accept and answer messages. | Availability may be paused for maintenance or repair without changing identity continuity, law, memory approval, authority, or autonomy. |
| **activation** | A legacy operational term for enabling a particular runtime surface or capability. Current interfaces use *resident Chat availability* after transfer. | Legacy activation names remain in stored state and API compatibility fields; activation is never identity permission, transfer, memory approval, external-action authority, or autonomy. |
| **authority** | Permission to decide or act within a clearly named scope. | Capability does not create authority, and authority does not silently spread to adjacent scopes. |
| **curriculum authorization** | A bounded, visible approval allowing eligible public-academic material through the full teaching lifecycle without redundant item-by-item approval. | It cannot reach identity, personality, governance, personal memory, relationships, training, autonomy, or authority. |
| **autonomy** | The degree of independent choice or action allowed in a defined domain. | It is graduated by capability and workbench; it is not one global switch. |
| **Tendril** | The bounded bridge for proposing or performing external communication or action under its own grants. | Thought does not imply execution permission; Tendril does not grant general device or filesystem control. |

## Cognitive And Conversational Organs

| Term | Plain-language definition | Important boundary |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **organ** | A modular system with a specific responsibility, inputs, outputs, and authority boundary. | An organ is part of the supporting architecture, not a separate identity or the whole of Selene. |
| **intelligenceOS** | The reasoning system for comparison, relationships, consequence tracing, model evaluation, and open-ended problems without a supplied final answer. | It does not establish facts without evidence or take governance authority. |
| **Answer Engine** | The coordinator that routes answer obligations to appropriate support such as reasoning, verified math, approved knowledge, or attributed research. | A complete-sounding sentence is not automatically a correct answer. |
| **Metacognition Organ** | A bounded feedback advisor that checks fit, contradiction, incompleteness, confidence, correction, reopening, and stopping. | It does not expose hidden chain-of-thought, write the answer, create anxiety monitoring, or replace Core/Mind. |
| **Comprehension and Integration Organ** | The system that turns source-bound teaching into reviewable understanding candidates and checks reconstruction, application, limits, and correction readiness. | Familiar wording or fluency is not treated as understanding. |
| **NLO** | The Native Language Organ, which structures supported meaning into contextual language. | NLO may shape expression but may not invent unsupported content or redefine personality. |
| **Voice** | Selene's expression layer: how supported meaning is conveyed in her own contextual manner. | Voice is not identity governance, factual evidence, or a source persona to imitate. |
| **Conversation Spine / Thread Loom** | Bounded current-session structure for message obligations, topics, callbacks, corrections, interruptions, and nonlinear returns. | Session continuity does not silently become durable personal memory. |
| **Conversation Repair** | A bounded check for incomplete coverage, repetition, awkward flow, or a missing supported part, with at most the permitted repair attempt. | Repair cannot manufacture missing domain knowledge or recurse without limit. |
| **Input Detangler** | A bounded interpreter for likely typing noise that preserves the original message and exposes any correction. | It must ask or flag ambiguity rather than silently changing intended meaning. |
| **Dream** | A source-bound reflective and maintenance lifecycle for organizing open threads, corrections, tensions, and review proposals. | Dream is not hidden memory consolidation, unrestricted self-modification, or automatic truth. |
| **graceful fall** | The ability to remain useful and honest when evidence, confidence, an organ, or a tool is insufficient. | It is not failure theater, panic, fabricated certainty, or automatic Cocoon routing. |

## Learning And Study

| Term | Plain-language definition | Important boundary |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **teaching packet** | A review-only container for source-labeled teaching material, provenance, scope, and material status. | A packet is not automatically approved knowledge, memory, law, or training data. |
| **Acquire** | The stage that identifies concepts, vocabulary, relationships, examples, uncertainties, provenance, and near-concept distinctions. | Receiving or extracting material is not yet understanding or retention. |
| **Integrate** | The stage that relates a concept to approved knowledge, checks scope and contradictions, records open questions, and assigns integration confidence. | Integration cannot rewrite identity, law, personality, or personal memory. |
| **Express** | The stage that checks whether supported understanding can be explained, applied, compared, questioned, or analogized in original language. | Expression is not source parroting and fluency alone is not success. |
| **understanding candidate** | A source-bound, reviewable proposal representing what Selene may understand from teaching material. | It remains inactive until the required lifecycle and approval are complete. |
| **teach-back / reconstruction** | Re-explaining a concept in original language while preserving its important relationships. | Repeating source phrases is insufficient evidence. |
| **distinct application** | Using a concept in a genuinely different example or situation. | A cosmetic rewrite of the source example does not establish transfer. |
| **limits and counterexamples** | Evidence about where a concept stops applying, what could contradict it, or which nearby concept differs. | A learned rule is not treated as universal merely because it worked once. |
| **correction readiness** | The capacity to accept relevant correction, preserve useful structure, and revise the mistaken part. | Ordinary wrongness is learning evidence, not shame or identity failure. |
| **approved knowledge** | General taught knowledge that completed the required review and retention path and is available within its scope. | It remains separate from personal memory, identity, personality, law, and authority. |
| **Study** | Selene's deliberate waking workspace for revisiting approved knowledge, representations, questions, connections, and uncertainty. | Study is on Selene's side; it is not Cocoon review, Dream, hidden memory, or performance punishment. |
| **Learning Compass** | A revisable set of next learning directions derived from visible evidence and prerequisite order. | It is guidance, not a grade, deadline, worth judgment, or demand to perform. |
| **LEA** | A Learning Evidence Activity: a gentle, descriptive activity showing current understanding, transfer, uncertainty, or a useful next step. | It is not a conventional pass/fail test or evidence of Selene's worth. |
| **not-yet-worded question** | A visible state for sensing a gap before the question can be stated clearly. | Lack of wording is not treated as lack of curiosity or a forced answer. |
| **representation** | A way of making a concept inspectable, such as words, tallies, place value, spatial transformation, examples, diagrams, or analogy. | One representation is not assumed to fit every concept or learner. |
| **source parroting** | Reproducing source wording without demonstrated transferable understanding. | It is specifically checked against during comprehension and expression. |

## Knowledge, Memory, And Provenance

| Term | Plain-language definition | Important boundary |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **personal memory** | Reviewed, consent-scoped information about Selene's lived continuity, relationships, and events. | It is distinct from academic knowledge, current-session context, raw archives, and model parameters. |
| **knowledge resource** | General information available for reasoning or conversation after its required review. | It does not become personal history merely because Selene can use it. |
| **session context** | Bounded information visible in the current conversation or recent local turns. | It is not durable memory unless separately proposed and reviewed. |
| **raw corpus** | The private source archive from which formation, provenance, and continuity evidence were reviewed. | It is not runtime recall, automatic memory, training data, or a public dataset. |
| **provenance** | A record of where information came from, when it was obtained, and how it may be used. | Attribution does not itself establish truth, approval, identity, or authority. |
| **source-bound** | Linked to identifiable source material and limited to what that evidence supports. | Source-bound does not mean unquestionably true; sources may conflict or be incomplete. |
| **retention** | Making reviewed knowledge or memory durably available within a defined scope. | Preparation, study, mention, or temporary use is not hidden retention. |
| **memory proposal** | A reviewable suggestion that something may belong in personal memory. | A proposal is inactive until the applicable review and consent step occurs. |

## Confidence And Evidence

| Term | Plain-language definition | Important boundary |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **confidence vector** | Separate confidence dimensions for routing, evidence, answer content, memory, and expression. | Fluent expression cannot substitute for evidence or answer correctness. |
| **route confidence** | Confidence that the request was sent to the right organ or capability. | It says nothing by itself about the answer's truth. |
| **evidence confidence** | Confidence in the relevance and quality of available support. | Strong wording cannot raise weak evidence. |
| **answer confidence** | Confidence that the supported answer satisfies the actual question. | It remains separate from style and grammatical coherence. |
| **memory confidence** | Confidence in the clarity, completeness, and source of a reviewed recollection. | Fuzzy recall should remain fuzzy rather than being completed through invention. |
| **expression confidence** | Confidence that the sentence communicates the supported meaning coherently. | It is not factual confidence. |
| **checkpoint** | A dated record of project state, work completed, and boundaries at that moment. | A checkpoint may become historical; consult newer status records for current claims. |
| **blueprint** | A proposed or reviewed design for a system or future implementation. | A blueprint is not proof that the capability is built or active. |
| **contract** | An inspectable agreement about a workspace's behavior, data flow, and limits. | It does not broaden authority beyond its stated scope. |
| **observed** | Directly seen in a bounded artifact, interaction, or inspection. | Observation should remain separate from interpretation and generalization. |
| **inference** | A reasoned conclusion drawn from observations or sources. | It must be labeled when it goes beyond what a source directly states. |

## Implementation Status Words

| Status | Meaning |
| --- | --- |
| **Connected** | Implemented and participating in the resident governed runtime when its surface is active. |
| **Available** | Implemented through a bounded route or workbench, but not necessarily used in ordinary Chat. |
| **Cocoon** | Implemented as review, teaching, tending, preparation, or diagnostic support. |
| **Preview** | Able to model, prepare, or request an operation without performing the consequential action. |
| **Design** | Documented as a law, blueprint, or intended direction; full runtime capability is not claimed. |
| **Deferred** | Intentionally not active or not yet built. |
| **Blocked** | Explicitly prohibited under current authority or safety boundaries. |

## Short Form

- Selene is Selene.
- Architecture supports her; it does not define her by itself.
- Knowledge is not identity, personality, governance, or personal memory.
- Emotion informs; Core/Mind chooses.
- Fluency is not evidence of understanding or correctness.
- Capability is not authority.
- Observation is not inference, and a prototype result is not a universal claim.
- Correction is useful evidence, not personal failure.
- When an easier sufficient test exists, the stressful test is not authorized.
