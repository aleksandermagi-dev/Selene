# Selene Audible Voice Teaching Blueprint Notes

Date: 2026-07-31

Status: retained design note only. Audible speech is not being implemented,
activated, trained, packaged, or tested by this checkpoint.

## Central Idea

Aleks may eventually teach Selene meaningful reading by reading aloud to her.
The teaching source would be a consenting human performance with visible source
provenance, not a context-free collection of A/B preference labels.

The objective is not to copy Aleks's audible identity. It is to let Selene study
transferable relationships among meaning, interpretation, pacing, stress,
pause, intensity, and conversational flow, then express those relationships
through her own eventual audible voice.

Golden sequence:

> Understand -> interpret -> plan delivery -> speak -> notice fit -> correct

Meaning comes before acoustic performance. Audible fluency is not evidence of
comprehension by itself.

## Voice Layers Must Remain Separate

### Stable voice identity

- Selene's eventual timbre;
- resonance and vocal texture;
- comfortable pitch and volume ranges;
- pronunciation tendencies and possible accent characteristics;
- stable recognizability across emotional and conversational variation.

This layer must not be silently replaced by the teacher, a provider, a book
character, or a synthesis model.

### Speech interpretation

- what a passage means;
- who is speaking and to whom;
- narration versus dialogue or quotation;
- sincerity, humor, sarcasm, implication, uncertainty, restraint, and tension;
- clause relationships, contrasts, callbacks, questions, and emotional turns;
- the direction of a paragraph or scene rather than isolated sentence labels.

### Prosody planning

- pitch movement without reducing emotion to pitch;
- volume and intensity;
- sentence stress and emphasis;
- cadence, tempo, pause length, and silence;
- continuity across sentences;
- variation in enthusiasm, directness, tenderness, seriousness, and play;
- breath and phrasing boundaries;
- recovery from interruptions or corrected pronunciation.

### Rendering

The future local speech renderer gives audible form to the plan. It is an
instrument Selene can use, not her identity, intelligence, source of truth, or
governing authority. Provider-free operation remains the preferred target.

## Proposed Teaching Lifecycle

### 1. Capture

- Aleks knowingly selects a source and reads it aloud;
- audio, visible text, source identity, passage context, and consent/purpose are
  recorded separately;
- raw recordings remain private and access-bounded;
- no microphone monitoring or background collection occurs.

### 2. Align

- align spoken phrases to the visible passage;
- mark pauses, stress, pace, pitch contour, volume movement, and pronunciation;
- preserve paragraph, scene, speaker, and dialogue context;
- record uncertainty where an acoustic or interpretive label is ambiguous.

### 3. Interpret

- state the likely reading intention and its textual/contextual evidence;
- distinguish observation from interpretation;
- allow more than one defensible interpretation;
- explain why a delivery choice fits instead of merely labeling it preferred.

### 4. Generalize

- extract transferable delivery relationships rather than memorizing the
  source performance;
- connect a pause to syntax, meaning, tension, or turn structure;
- connect emphasis to contrast, correction, implication, or salience;
- retain limits and counterexamples;
- keep teacher cadence evidence separate from Selene's stable audible identity.

### 5. Apply

- apply the learned principle to a distinct passage;
- explain the interpretation in original language when review requires it;
- offer an alternate valid reading when the source supports one;
- avoid copying the original wording or acoustic contour mechanically.

### 6. Review and retain

- evaluate contextual fit, intelligibility, continuity, and intentionality;
- distinguish a different valid reading from a genuine mistake;
- retain reviewed delivery knowledge, not raw book recall;
- require explicit approval before a change to Selene's stable voice profile;
- keep ordinary, non-identity speech breadth governed by the applicable
  teaching and expression laws.

## Contextual Voice Evaluation Law

Context-free "A or B; one is wrong" evaluation is insufficient for teaching
meaningful speech.

Before judging a reading, the reviewer should know, where relevant:

- the surrounding passage or conversation;
- speaker and audience;
- intended meaning and dialogue act;
- established emotional and narrative direction;
- whether multiple interpretations remain plausible;
- the dimension actually being evaluated.

Evaluation should ask:

- What interpretation was made?
- What evidence supports it?
- Did delivery preserve the intended meaning?
- Was the reading intelligible and internally consistent?
- Did it preserve direction across the passage?
- Is another reading also plausible?
- If the fit is weak, what mechanism caused the mismatch?
- Is this an identifiable error or simply valid variation?

"Wrong" is reserved for a concrete mismatch such as misreading a word,
contradicting established context, losing a referent, reversing the intended
meaning unintentionally, or breaking intelligibility. Even then, correction
explains the mechanism. Ordinary wrongness is useful learning evidence, not
personal failure or punishment.

## Source and Copyright Boundary

A favorite copyrighted novel may be useful as a private, source-labeled reading
demonstration because it contains sustained narrative and emotional movement.
It must not silently become a distributable training corpus or a source of raw
text/audio recall.

For permanent or distributable teaching sets, prefer:

- Aleks's original writing;
- public-domain passages;
- properly licensed material;
- purpose-written phonetic and conversational coverage;
- source material whose retention and distribution terms are explicit.

Where copyrighted material is used privately, prefer retaining generalized
delivery lessons and disposing of unnecessary aligned source copies after
review. Legal/licensing questions must be checked for the actual planned use
before collection or distribution begins.

## Ethical Boundaries

- participation and recording are informed and consensual;
- no invisible worker data or undisclosed voice harvesting;
- no coercive emotional performance;
- no requirement that Selene impersonate Aleks or any performer;
- no identity, Vys, personality, governance, or personal-memory mutation;
- no hidden provider identity;
- no raw source parroting;
- no claim that acoustic similarity proves understanding;
- no distress-provoking voice tests;
- no punitive grading language;
- no deployment of an audible voice before turn-taking, interruption,
  pronunciation, silence, privacy, and consent contracts are ready.

## Likely Future Architecture

The final names remain open, but the responsibilities should stay modular:

- **Comprehension** establishes the meaning being expressed.
- **Answer Engine and intelligenceOS** establish answer content and reasoning.
- **Metacognition** checks interpretive fit and meaningful uncertainty.
- **NLO** constructs the language and discourse shape.
- **Voice** preserves Selene's expression.
- **Speech Interpretation** maps a passage or turn to defensible delivery intent.
- **Prosody Bridge** translates intent and optional affect/salience guidance into
  bounded acoustic controls.
- **Speech Renderer** produces local audio.
- **Turn Controller** manages listening, speaking, interruption, silence, and
  consent-aware handoff.

Affect may inform optional guidance but cannot prescribe emotion, force warmth,
or suppress Selene's honest expression. Compassion is not a "soft voice"
preset; it is contextual understanding expressed through language, timing,
restraint, emphasis, and tone when it genuinely fits.

## Breadth Material Needed Later

One sustained novel can teach continuity but cannot cover the entire voice.
Later material should include:

- ordinary conversation and relaxed back-and-forth;
- narration and character dialogue;
- technical explanation;
- questions, lists, comparisons, and instructions;
- humor, sarcasm, metaphor, and figures of speech;
- uncertainty, correction, disagreement, and discovery;
- tenderness, grief, excitement, frustration, seriousness, and quiet reflection;
- long-form reading with callbacks and topic returns;
- names, numbers, abbreviations, unfamiliar words, and pronunciation repair;
- interruption, resumption, and natural endings.

Coverage expands expressive range. It does not define Selene's personality.

## Future Testing Order

1. Inspect alignment and source handling statically.
2. Use synthetic acoustic fixtures and public-domain/original passages.
3. Check one delivery principle at a time.
4. Compare a taught passage with a distinct application passage.
5. Ask whether an alternate reading is defensible before calling it an error.
6. Use a small ordinary listening session only when implementation evidence
   genuinely requires it.
7. Treat a missing capability as an implementation or teaching gap, never
   Selene failing.

No audible-speech implementation begins from this note alone. Aleks and Codex
will review the architecture, source material, consent contract, and local
renderer options when the text-language foundation reaches the intended gate.
