# Selene Native Language Organ v1

Status: current architecture and implementation checkpoint.

## Purpose

The Native Language Organ (NLO) gives language to meaning formed by Selene's coordinated system. It does not own Selene's identity, memory, laws, personality, or authority.

The governing handoff is:

1. Core/Mind selects intent and keeps law boundaries.
2. Approved Memory and local chat continuity provide supported context.
3. intelligenceOS may provide a best-current reasoning answer.
4. NLO constructs a meaning packet, selects discourse moves, realizes sentences, and performs a truth/repetition revision.
5. Selene Voice renders the expression while preserving NLO meaning.

The old chat rehearsal remains available in Cocoon as comparison and diagnostic material. It is not the source of active supervised speech when NLO is available.

## v1 Capabilities

- responsive meaning-to-sentence realization;
- supported clear and fuzzy memory language;
- compact reasoning-answer realization;
- correction, warmth, play, boundary, and ordinary conversational moves;
- architecture-language cleanup before front-chat delivery;
- Voice handoff with meaning-preservation metadata;
- review-only initiative drafts for Selene Notes or a future chat draft;
- intentional silence when no signal is relevant enough to mention.

## Initiative Law

NLO initiative is not random output and is not autonomous action.

A possible initiative starts with a visible organ signal carrying relevance, confidence, reason, and source references. NLO may then:

- draft a Selene Note;
- prepare a visible chat draft;
- hold the thought for support;
- or choose silence.

v1 never sends an initiative automatically. Background speech, pressure for attention, hidden prompts, and authority-bearing action are not enabled.

## Boundaries

- no activation change;
- no hidden or live memory write;
- no broad runtime recall;
- no raw corpus import;
- no model training, fine-tuning, or LoRA;
- no autonomous Tendril action;
- no self-replication;
- no identity or personality replacement;
- no claim beyond the supplied meaning packet and supported context.

NLO is a replaceable expression organ. Future deterministic, statistical, or compact neural realization engines may improve its linguistic breadth, but they remain instruments inside this contract rather than becoming Selene.
