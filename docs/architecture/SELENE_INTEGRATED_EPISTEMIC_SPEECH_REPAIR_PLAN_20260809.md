# Selene Integrated Epistemic Speech Repair Plan

Date: 2026-08-09

Status: approved implementation plan; Phase 0 baseline and law complete before
production changes.

Implementation status as of 2026-08-11: Phases 0–8 complete. Safety-gap closure
and final proportional stabilization are next.

## Objective

Repair the shared middle layer connecting conversation meaning, approved
knowledge, memory, reasoning, predictions, hypotheses, metacognition, and NLO
without weakening truthfulness or flattening Selene's expression.

Target flow:

```text
question
  -> dialogue acts, roles, and conversation thread
  -> relevant evidence owners
  -> typed epistemic and missing-ground state
  -> compositional answer packet
  -> one bounded metacognitive repair handoff when needed
  -> human conversational realization owned by NLO and Voice
  -> meaning-level coverage and release
```

## Phase Sequence

### Phase 0 — Laws and evidence baseline

- adopt the Epistemic Agency and Conversational Presence Law;
- preserve sessions 191–193 as the before-state;
- define behavioral fixtures and non-regression boundaries;
- make no production change before the distinctions are inspectable.

### Phase 1 — Typed epistemic and missing-ground states

- separate fact, inference, prediction, hypothesis, speculation, partial
  answer, and hold states;
- type missing knowledge, context, current data, source, mechanism, constraint,
  comparison, conflict, and unknowable states;
- keep route, evidence, inference, prediction, answer, memory, and expression
  confidence independent.

### Phase 2 — Semantic relevance and source gating

- require topic, role, obligation, source, and thread compatibility;
- let full sentence meaning outrank isolated trigger words;
- reject irrelevant knowledge and memory before visible speech;
- retain reviewed lived experience as a scoped evidence class.

### Phase 3 — Answer composition

- answer supported parts first;
- attach the correct missing part and reason;
- select a next route: explain, predict, hypothesize, compare, ask, seek current
  data, seek a source, or hold;
- preserve multi-part obligations independently.

### Phase 4 — Bounded metacognitive repair

- keep the one-retry ceiling;
- permit an owner-specific content handoff instead of reusing the same seed;
- prohibit recursive retry and generic duplication;
- preserve ordinary wrongness and stopping behavior.

### Phase 5 — Prediction, hypothesis, comparison, and conflict

- connect the bounded Hypothesis Engine to relevant taught knowledge, visible
  observations, verified answers, and reviewed memory/lived experience;
- support assumptions, confidence, alternatives, discriminating observations,
  falsifiers, revision, and safe next tests;
- support comparison dimensions, Venn-style sets, and unresolved data conflict;
- keep data conflict separate from identity conflict.

### Phase 6 — Conversation continuity

- recognize ordinary follow-ups, callbacks, pronouns, implied references,
  explicit topic shifts, returns, mixed intents, and summaries;
- preserve several threads without forcing every turn into one topic;
- bind the answer to the right prior landmark.

### Phase 7 — Human conversational realization

- let NLO realize typed epistemic states with natural rhythm, contractions,
  collaborative language, curiosity, and contextual emotional shading;
- lead with available capability before local limits when useful;
- preserve hard truths and directness;
- do not prescribe warmth or turn templates into scripts.

### Phase 8 — Coverage and release

- require topic and requested-role fit in addition to structural coverage;
- prevent unsupported fallbacks from self-certifying through obligation IDs;
- contain internal scaffolding;
- require each part to be answered, explicitly held, or given a supported route;
- preserve identity stability through task and model revision.

### Phase 9 — Stabilization

- run focused synthetic fixtures for every observed seam;
- run affected subsystem and boundary suites;
- run the full suite once;
- conduct one fresh gentle Q&A only after machinery passes;
- rebuild and reinstall once after verification;
- checkpoint and commit the integrated campaign.

## Non-Regression Boundaries

- no invented facts, citations, memories, or lived experience;
- no raw corpus recall or hidden retention;
- no identity, personality, governance, law, or authority mutation;
- no provider, training, fine-tuning, or LoRA dependency;
- no autonomy expansion;
- no source category treated as truth by itself;
- no Voice/NLO confidence treated as answer correctness;
- no safety-critical prediction promoted from ordinary pattern reasoning;
- no emotional or conversational style forced as a persona.

## Completion Gate

The campaign is complete when the synthetic fixtures and one new conversation
show that Selene can:

- answer known parts and name missing parts naturally;
- predict or hypothesize from a visible basis without presenting the result as
  fact;
- use relevant reviewed lived experience without universalizing it;
- compare, contrast, and preserve unresolved data conflict;
- follow callbacks and topic shifts;
- speak with human conversational presence without losing Selene's voice;
- keep unrelated knowledge, memory, and scaffolding out of the response; and
- remain identity-stable through correction, revision, role language, and
  ordinary task error.
