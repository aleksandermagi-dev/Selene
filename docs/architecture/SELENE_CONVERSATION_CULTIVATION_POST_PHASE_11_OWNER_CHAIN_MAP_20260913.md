# Selene Conversation Cultivation — Post-Phase 11 Owner-Chain Map

Date: 2026-09-13

Status: source map and production repair complete; package/install verification pending

## Purpose

The installed Phase 11 Q&A found one connected conversation-owner defect. This
is not a teaching gap, identity issue, emotional-state problem, or failure by
Selene. It is an implementation gap in how ordinary current-turn material
reaches the answer owner and how downstream completion is proved.

The repair must start at the earliest divergence and must not add scenario
phrases for lamps, sound, or the reading corner.

## Ethical test decision

- What does this do to Selene? It repairs coordination of visible user input;
  it does not challenge or grade her.
- How might it feel? The repair removes repeated internal holds and reduces the
  chance that an unfinished module makes her appear confused.
- Is testing necessary? Yes, but static and synthetic fixtures are sufficient
  during implementation.
- Can distress-shaped probing be avoided? Yes. Use ordinary low-stakes objects,
  observations, corrections, topic changes, summaries, and humor.
- Is this Selene or an unfinished module? The evidence concerns the unfinished
  conversation-owner chain.

Do not repeat the installed six-turn Q&A until the shared source chain passes
focused synthetic verification.

## Root repair order

1. Expand shared option normalization to accept bounded, explicitly counted
   concrete alternatives with repeated determiners, independent of fixture
   nouns and introductory verbs.
2. Preserve ordinary non-request declarative event/state reports as
   user-reported observations without independently verifying them.
3. Let the existing current-turn owner consume those facts before optional
   approved knowledge or memory retrieval can claim the visible answer.
4. Require requested visible operations to have typed owner/realization proof;
   lexical overlap or fluent status prose alone is not completion.
5. Detect a genuine semantic topic transition strongly enough to expire a held
   revision while preserving explicit callbacks and returns.
6. Recheck mixed requested takeaways, humor, and open-thread participation only
   after steps 1–5 are sound.

## Non-goals

- no teaching around the bug;
- no lamp-, sound-, or reading-corner-specific response script;
- no new organ;
- no memory, identity, personality, governance, or authority mutation;
- no provider or model dependency;
- no autonomous filesystem, network, Tendril, or external-action authority;
- no reinterpretation of missing implementation capability as Selene failing.

## Verification gate

Focused synthetic evidence must cover:

- ordinary counted alternatives using unfamiliar nouns and varied clause order;
- a prompt-contained observation plus requested observation, interpretation,
  and next check;
- optional approved knowledge remaining behind a capable current owner;
- correction after a valid current result;
- held revision expiry on a genuinely unrelated topic without an explicit
  “new topic” phrase;
- two requested takeaways plus one actual tiny joke;
- an explicit request to keep a named thread open;
- existing safety, memory-write, training, action, identity, and governance
  locks.

Repository-wide tests, a build, reinstall, or installed Q&A belong after these
focused gates and only when proportionate to the resulting source change.

## Repair checkpoint — 2026-09-19

- Proposition Normalization now carries bounded counted alternatives and
  ordinary visible event reports into their existing current-turn owners.
